Rules & controls

Left-click to reveal; right-click to flag or unflag. Unflag a cell before revealing it. Your first reveal is safe.

Exactly one safe cell lies by +1 or −1. Flood fill follows true values, so a true zero expands even if its clue says 1.

You have 5 lives. Each newly revealed mine costs one life. Reveal every safe cell to win; finding the liar is optional. At the end, all mines and the liar's original clue are shown.
