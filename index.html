Rules & controls — meet the mischievous clue

One little fib. Exactly one safe cell shows a wrong number, off by exactly 1 (+1 or −1). Every other clue counts neighboring mines, including diagonals.

Clicks & notes. Left-click to reveal; right-click to flag or unflag a covered cell. Unflag it before revealing. Flags are just your own notes and are never checked. Your first reveal is safe; its neighbors might not be!

Watch what opens. Flood fill uses true values: a true-0 liar showing 1 still expands, while a true-1 liar showing 0 does not. That can be a clue! A liar displaying zero shows an explicit “0”; an ordinary, unverified true-0 cell is blank.

Check a clue. You have 3 verifications per game. Click Verify, then a revealed safe number (or blank) to show its true value with a permanent ✓ mark. Verify mode then turns off. Click Verify again to cancel for free. Covered cells, mines, and already verified cells use nothing and leave verify mode on. Verification never opens neighbors.

Room for mistakes. You start with 5 lives. Each newly revealed mine costs 1 life; the 5th distinct mine hit ends the game. Clicking a hit mine again costs nothing.

Clear the board. Reveal every non-mine cell to win. Finding the liar is optional; flagging mines alone does not win. At the end, all mines and both the liar's true and original displayed values are shown. New Game gives you a fresh start. Take your time!
